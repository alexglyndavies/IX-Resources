    Professional Development Programme 2026–2027¶

    For Schmidt AI in Science Fellows¶

    Programme at a glance
    When: October 2026 – June 2027
    Primary venue: I-X Conference Room
    Programme lead: Tracy Bussoli

    Upcoming events¶

    Developing and articulating your research vision¶

    Thursday 1 October 2026, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Thursday 22 October 2026 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    Fellowship and lectureship opportunities require applicants to communicate a compelling vision for their research. This two-hour session gives you space to step back from day-to-day work, consider where you want to take your research over the next five years, and test your ideas with other fellows.

    Through supportive questions and discussion, participants will help one another think creatively and ambitiously about their future research plans.

    You will leave with: a written first draft of your research vision.

    Lecturers coaching circle¶

    Tuesday 13 October 2026, 12:00–13:00 · Meeting Room 5
    Facilitated by: Tracy Bussoli

    A one-hour peer-coaching session for Schmidt Fellows who have secured lectureships.

    Coaching skills for research leaders¶

    Thursday 5 November 2026, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Thursday 19 November 2026, 15:00–17:00
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    For fellows and faculty who manage people—or expect to do so soon—this workshop introduces coaching as a practical management tool. You will explore what coaching is and the situations in which it can help the people you manage.

    After the workshop, you can opt into a peer-coaching circle to put your new skills into practice.

    Academic interviews¶

    Thursday 3 December 2026, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Thursday 7 January 2027, 15:00–17:00
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    This two-hour workshop complements Imperial's existing academic interview support, including mock interview practice from ECRI. You will learn how to prepare effectively, practise answering typical academic interview questions, and receive feedback from other fellows and academics with interview-panel experience.

    Funding proposals: peer review and feedback¶

    Thursday 4 February 2027, 14:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    Most fellows will write a funding proposal during their fellowship, whether for a fellowship or a research grant. In this workshop, volunteer Schmidt Fellows can have draft applications reviewed by their peers.

    Participants will examine two funding proposals and provide detailed feedback, developing their grant-writing skills while gaining valuable insight into the reviewer's perspective.

    On-demand support: A small review committee can also be convened when a grant application needs feedback.

    Lecturers coaching circle¶

    Tuesday 16 February 2027, 12:00–13:00 · Meeting Room 5
    Facilitated by: Tracy Bussoli

    A one-hour peer-coaching session for Schmidt Fellows who have secured lectureships.

    Managing your time effectively¶

    Thursday 4 March 2027, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    Academic life brings no shortage of competing demands. This two-hour workshop offers a chance to explore workload challenges with other fellows and connect day-to-day priorities to your wider research vision and life.

    You will leave with: a clearer view of which tasks matter, which can be delegated, and which you should say no to.

    Managing master's students 1: preparing¶

    Date: To be confirmed · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli and Alex Glyn-Davies

    A practical introduction to managing master's students. The workshop covers marking schemes and uses case studies to explore common supervisory situations and ways to apply your coaching skills.

    Managing master's students 2: reflecting and improving¶

    Date: To be confirmed · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli and Alex Glyn-Davies

    A small-group session in which fellows can reflect on their experience of managing master's students. Bring challenges for discussion, identify improvements, and celebrate and share what has worked well.

    Working towards managing your first research group¶

    Thursday 6 May 2027 · SALC, South Kensington Campus (room TBC)
    Delivered by: Stefanie Edler-Wollstein and Tracy Bussoli

    Moving from contributing to research projects to directing an independently funded programme requires more than an excellent research record. This one-day course for new and aspiring principal investigators uses real-life examples to help you create a personal plan for managing your first research group.

    You will:

    • identify your strengths, development areas, values and motivations;
    • compare different leadership and management approaches;
    • explore how to attract and recruit the right people;
    • learn practical techniques for prioritising and managing your time; and
    • define and develop a research profile for your laboratory or group.

    Managing your impostor feelings¶

    Thursday 13 May 2027, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    Working in environments where academic excellence is highly visible can understandably lead to periods of self-doubt. Taking place during Mental Health Awareness Week, this two-hour workshop will help you reflect on those moments and develop tools to keep impostor feelings in perspective.

    Exploring careers beyond academia¶

    Thursday 3 June 2027, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli, joined by two Schmidt alumni working in industry (TBC)

    For fellows considering careers beyond academia, the range of roles and organisations can feel overwhelming. This two-hour workshop explores career options, ways to identify suitable companies, and approaches to applying.

    Two Schmidt alumni working in industry will join online for 30 minutes to share their experiences of job hunting and life beyond academia.

    Lecturers coaching circle¶

    Tuesday 8 June 2027, 12:00–13:00 · Meeting Room 5
    Facilitated by: Tracy Bussoli

    A one-hour peer-coaching session for Schmidt Fellows who have secured lectureships.

    CVs and interviews for careers beyond academia¶

    Thursday 17 June 2027, 15:00–17:00 · I-X Conference Room
    Delivered by: Tracy Bussoli

    Applying for roles beyond academia calls for a different style of CV and interview preparation. This two-hour workshop will help you tailor a CV to a specific role, highlight and evidence relevant skills, anticipate common interview questions, and structure persuasive answers using examples from your experience.

    Fellows coaching circles¶

    Date: To be confirmed
    Facilitated by: Tracy Bussoli

    One-hour peer-coaching sessions for fellows interested in developing their coaching practice. Participation will be established during the Coaching skills for research leaders workshop.
